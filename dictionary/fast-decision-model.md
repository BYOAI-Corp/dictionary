A fast decision model answers narrow questions inside a check, such as whether a passage names a date or which of three clauses applies. The [[Verdict]] itself is worked out in code from those answers, and it still needs [[Independent agreement]] before anything is [[VERIFIED]].

## Heard in the wild

- Why not just ask it whether the claim is true?
- Broad questions invite guesses. Narrow ones can be checked.
