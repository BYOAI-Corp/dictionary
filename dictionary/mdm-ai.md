MDM AI is BYOAI's AI-assisted management for company phones and laptops: enrolment, policy and device posture checks, run by an assistant that works through the device-management tools a company already has.

## Heard in the wild

- Can MDM AI tell us which devices are out of policy?
- Yes. Ask it, and it checks the devices' posture against your policy.
