Each record points to the one before it and is signed, so any later edit or deletion breaks the chain where it happened. One chain covers verdicts, [[Handoff]]s and payments, and a [[Sealed release]] uses the same idea for published files.

## Heard in the wild

- Can you prove nobody changed the result?
- Check the chain of custody; an edit would break it.

## Avoid

`audit chain`, `evidence log`, `audit trail`; say Chain of custody.
