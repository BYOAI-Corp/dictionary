Modes trade speed against oversight. Looser modes are only safe inside a strong [[Sandbox]] with good [[Automated check]]s. The mode decides when a [[Permission request]] appears for a [[Tool call]].

## Heard in the wild

- Can I let it run without asking?
- Inside the sandbox and on a branch, yes. Never on your own home folder.
