A grounded claim points at the exact words in its [[Source]] that support it, its [[Supporting quote]]. Grounding is what separates a checked statement from a plausible one, and it is the main defence against [[Hallucination]].

## Heard in the wild

- It sounds right. Is that enough?
- No. Show where the source says it; that's grounding.
