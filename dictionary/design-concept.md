Agents build faster than people can redirect them, so agreeing on the concept first saves the most rework. [[Grilling]] and a written [[Spec]] are the usual ways to pin it down.

## Heard in the wild

- It built something, but not what I meant.
- We skipped the concept. Grill first next time.
