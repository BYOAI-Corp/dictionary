Hallucination Token Credits is a proposed standard: when an AI's output is shown to be wrong, for example by an [[UNGROUNDED]] verdict with a [[Verification receipt]], the user gets back the tokens they paid for. Claims would settle through the [[Verification Clearinghouse]].

## Heard in the wild

- Who decides the output was wrong?
- The verification receipt, settled by an independent clearinghouse.
