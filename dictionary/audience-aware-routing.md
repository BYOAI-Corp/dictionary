The same claim can matter a little or a lot depending on who relies on it. Audience-aware routing raises the bar for high-stakes readers, such as a court or a regulator, and works together with [[Complexity routing]] and the [[Pre-generation gate]] to pick the path before anything is checked.

## Heard in the wild

- Why did this check take the slow path?
- The answer is going into a filing, so routing chose the strictest path.
