A good handoff carries pointers to [[Primary source]]s and a short [[Handoff artifact]], not a retelling of the whole [[Session]]. It is the main way to keep work inside the [[Smart zone]].

## Heard in the wild

- How do I move this to the laptop?
- Write a handoff file, commit it, open a fresh session there.

## Avoid

Handing off a long summary instead of pointers to the files that hold the truth.
