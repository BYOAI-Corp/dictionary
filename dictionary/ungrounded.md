UNGROUNDED means the [[Source]] doesn't support the claim, or says something different. In a per-claim report it shows as HALLUCINATED, a [[Claim result]]. It's the result that catches a [[Hallucination]] before anyone relies on it.

## Heard in the wild

- The citation came back UNGROUNDED. Now what?
- Fix the claim or find the source that supports it; don't ship it as is.
