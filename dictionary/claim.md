A claim is the unit the [[Verifier]] checks: one fact, one sentence, in the writer's own words. Long passages are split into claims first, and the verifier may split a claim further into [[Sub-claim]]s, each checked against the [[Source]].

## Heard in the wild

- Can I just send the whole memo?
- Split it into one-fact sentences first; whole paragraphs mostly come back ABSTAIN.
