A [[Harness]] gives a [[Model]] tools and rules; a verification harness adds the checking. The model, often a [[Frontier model]], answers; the harness sends its claims to the [[Verifier]] and returns only what the evidence supports. Swapping the model leaves the checks in place.

## Heard in the wild

- Do we need a better model or a better harness?
- Usually the harness: it decides what's checked before anyone sees it.
