A frontier model is a large hosted [[Model]] from a [[Model provider]]. In BYOAI it drafts answers and handles the hardest checks under [[Complexity routing]], but a [[Verdict]] always comes from the [[Verifier]], never from the model's own opinion.

## Heard in the wild

- If the frontier model is so good, why check it?
- Good isn't grounded. The check shows where its answer came from.
