The BYOAI router sends each task to the right [[Model]] or [[Model provider]], hosted or on the client's own hardware, and works with [[Complexity routing]] to keep costs down. Every checked result still goes through the [[Verifier]].

## Heard in the wild

- Can we switch model providers later?
- Yes. Change the route, not the checks.
