UNCHECKED means no check ran, for example because the [[Verifier]] was unavailable or no source was given. It's never shown as green and never counted as a pass, under the [[Evidence-only green]] rule.

## Heard in the wild

- The verifier was down. Can we mark these as fine for now?
- No. They stay UNCHECKED until they're checked.
