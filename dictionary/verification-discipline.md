Every check follows the same order: a [[Claim]], a named [[Source]], then a [[Verdict]] issued by the [[Verifier]]. Each statement in a report is marked as checked or not checked, and [[Evidence-only green]] means nothing turns green without a [[Supporting quote]].

## Heard in the wild

- How do I know a green result wasn't just the model agreeing with itself?
- The discipline forbids it: green needs a quote from the source, found by the verifier, not a model's say-so.
