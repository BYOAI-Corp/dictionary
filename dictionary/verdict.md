A verdict is one of four results: [[VERIFIED]], [[UNGROUNDED]], [[ABSTAIN]] or [[UNCHECKED]]. Only the [[Verifier]] issues one, it rolls up every [[Claim result]] beneath it, and it always comes with a [[Verification receipt]].

## Heard in the wild

- The model said it's fine. Is that a verdict?
- No. Only the verifier issues verdicts.
