Every [[Verdict]] leaves a verification receipt: a signed record with an ID, a sequence number and a hash, linked to the receipts before it in a [[Chain of custody]]. Anyone can check a receipt later with the open-source [[Receipt chain library]], without trusting BYOAI.

## Heard in the wild

- How do I prove this was checked last month?
- Show the receipt; its place in the chain shows when, and that nothing changed since.
