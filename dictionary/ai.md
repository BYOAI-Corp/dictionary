"AI" covers everything from spam filters to chess engines, so on its own it says little. When people talk about AI coding they mean a [[Model]] trained on text and code, reached through a [[Harness]] such as a chat app or a coding [[Agent]].

Name the actual thing when it matters: which model, which harness, which provider.

## Heard in the wild

- Did the AI write this?
- Claude wrote it in Claude Code, from the spec in the ticket.

## Avoid

Using "the AI" as if it were one fixed thing; two models in two harnesses behave very differently.
