When a [[Claim]] holds more than one fact, the [[Verifier]] splits it into sub-claims. Each gets its own [[Claim result]] and [[Supporting quote]], so a report shows exactly which part held and which part did not.

## Heard in the wild

- The claim came back UNGROUNDED. Which part was wrong?
- Open the sub-claims: one held, one had no support in the source.
