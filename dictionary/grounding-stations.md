Grounding Stations are the checkpoints an AI answer passes through before anyone relies on it: the [[Verifier]], the [[Verification harness]] and the receipts they leave. The idea is that an answer is grounded at a station, not trusted on arrival.

## Heard in the wild

- Where does an answer get checked?
- At a grounding station, before it reaches you.
