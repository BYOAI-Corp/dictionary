The Verification Clearinghouse is where AI agents pay for checks and receive [[Verification receipt]]s, so a payment and the verdict it bought are one record. BYOAI never holds, pools or directs anyone else's money. It's also where [[Hallucination Token Credits]] would be settled.

## Heard in the wild

- Does BYOAI hold the payment?
- No. Payments go through the payment provider; BYOAI only issues the receipt.
