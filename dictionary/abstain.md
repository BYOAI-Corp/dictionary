ABSTAIN means the check can't settle the claim: the [[Source]] is silent, contradicts itself, the claim is opinion, or the judges split under [[Independent agreement]]. It's an answer, not an error, and it hands the call to a person.

## Heard in the wild

- Is ABSTAIN a fail?
- No. It means a person needs to decide.
