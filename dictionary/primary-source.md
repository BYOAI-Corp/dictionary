term: Primary source
section: handoffs
order: 46
short: The thing itself: the code, the test output, the actual document. Not someone's account of it.
audience: public

A fresh agent reading the primary source gets the truth with full detail. A [[Secondary source]] such as a summary is cheaper but can be wrong or out of date. Handoffs should point to primary sources wherever they can.

## Heard in the wild

- The summary says the tests pass.
- Run them. The output is the primary source.
