Rather than loading every document, the [[Context]] holds names and one-line descriptions; the full text is read when relevant. [[Skill]]s work this way, and [[Context pointer]]s are the links that make it possible.

## Heard in the wild

- How do I give it all our docs?
- A one-page index with pointers; it opens what it needs.
