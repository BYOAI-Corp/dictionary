Evidence-only green is BYOAI's rule that [[VERIFIED]] is issued only when evidence was found and quoted. Silence, a missing objection or an unavailable checker never turns into green; they become [[ABSTAIN]] or [[UNCHECKED]].

## Heard in the wild

- Nothing flagged it, so it's green?
- No. Green needs evidence, not silence.
