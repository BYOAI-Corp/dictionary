Compaction frees room in the [[Context window]] but turns detail into a [[Secondary source]]. Do it at a phase boundary, not mid-task, and keep the important facts in files. [[Autocompact]] is the harness doing it for you.

## Heard in the wild

- Should I compact now?
- After the tickets are written, not in the middle of the debugging.
