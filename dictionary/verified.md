VERIFIED means the [[Source]] supports the claim word for word, shown by its [[Supporting quote]], and two independent judges agreed. It is the only green result, under the [[Evidence-only green]] rule.

## Heard in the wild

- Is 'likely true' the same as VERIFIED?
- No. VERIFIED needs the quote; likely is ABSTAIN.
