The receipt chain library is BYOAI's open-source code for writing and checking [[Verification receipt]]s and their [[Chain of custody]]. Because the format and the checker are open, a client can confirm a receipt on their own machine.

## Heard in the wild

- Do I have to trust your server to check a receipt?
- No. Run the open-source checker yourself.
