Two judges from different model families must both support a claim before it is [[VERIFIED]]. When they split, a third reads both arguments, but that tie-break can't turn a result green: it stays [[ABSTAIN]] and goes to a person. Different families make it unlikely that both share the same blind spot.

## Heard in the wild

- The judges disagreed. Who won?
- Nobody. A split stays ABSTAIN until a person decides.
