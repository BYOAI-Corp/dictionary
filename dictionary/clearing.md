Clear when nothing in the current [[Context]] matters to what comes next, or when a [[Ticket]] or [[Spec]] on disk already holds what the next step needs. Anything not written down is gone; that is the point.

## Heard in the wild

- Do I lose the plan if I clear?
- Only if it isn't in the spec file. It is, so clear.
