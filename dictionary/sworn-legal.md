Sworn Legal is BYOAI's legal product. AI-written legal work, such as memos, briefs and contract summaries, is checked claim by claim against its [[Source]]s by the [[Verifier]], and every result comes with a [[Verification receipt]].

## Heard in the wild

- Does Sworn write the brief for me?
- It checks what was written, yours or an AI's, against the sources.
