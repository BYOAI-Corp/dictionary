The model produces what is likely, not what is checked. Gaps in its [[Parametric knowledge]], a stale [[Knowledge cutoff]], or missing [[Contextual knowledge]] all invite it. The cure is grounding: put the real source in context and verify each claim against it.

## Heard in the wild

- It cited a case I can't find.
- Then treat the citation as invented until a source shows it.

## Avoid

Calling every mistake a hallucination; a wrong answer from bad context is a context problem.
