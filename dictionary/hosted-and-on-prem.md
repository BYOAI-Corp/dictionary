Every BYOAI product runs in two ways: hosted by BYOAI, or on the client's own hardware for data that can't leave the building. The checks and the [[Verification receipt]]s are the same either way. [[Sworn Legal Enterprise]] is the usual on-prem case.

## Heard in the wild

- Can we start hosted and move on-prem later?
- Yes. The same checks run in both.
