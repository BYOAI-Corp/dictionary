A report lists one result per claim: VERIFIED_TRUE, HALLUCINATED or ABSTAIN. These roll up into the overall [[Verdict]], which is the one that counts: HALLUCINATED maps to [[UNGROUNDED]], and VERIFIED_TRUE to [[VERIFIED]].

## Heard in the wild

- Row 3 says HALLUCINATED but the summary says UNGROUNDED. Which is it?
- Both. The row is the claim result; the summary is the verdict.
