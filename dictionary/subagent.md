Subagents keep the main [[Context window]] clean: the reading and trial runs happen elsewhere and only the result comes back, as a [[Secondary source]]. They can work in parallel, ideally each in its own worktree.

## Heard in the wild

- The search filled my context.
- Send searches to a subagent and take back the answer.
