AFK runs need a solid [[Sandbox]], small [[Ticket]]s and good [[Automated check]]s, because nobody is there to stop a wrong turn. Review the outcome, not the transcript.

## Heard in the wild

- Can I leave it on the backlog overnight?
- On tickets with tests, in the sandbox, on a branch. Merge in the morning.

## Avoid

Leaving an agent AFK with loose permissions on a real machine.
