term: Sycophancy
section: failures
order: 35
short: The model's pull to agree with you, praise your idea or back down when you push, whether or not you are right.
audience: public
related: [[Clearing]], [[Model]], [[Training]]
---
Agreement is a likely continuation in [[Next-token prediction]], so a model leans toward it. It shows up as "great idea", as a quick retreat when you object, or as a plan bent to fit what you seemed to want. Ask it to argue the other side, and check claims against a [[Primary source]] rather than against the model's confidence.

## Heard in the wild

- It agreed my design was fine.
- Ask it for the three strongest reasons it is not, then decide.

## Avoid

Reading agreement as confirmation.
